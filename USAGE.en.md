## Getting Started

KODOCO answers the question **"Where should I take the kids today?"** for Shiga Prefecture, Japan.

- Free
- 168 spots in Shiga (growing)
- Phone, tablet, or PC

On iPhone, **Safari → "Add to Home Screen"** turns it into a regular-feeling app (PWA).

---

## 1. Initial Setup

### Add your kids

1. Open Settings (gear icon, top right)
2. "Children" → "+ Add"
3. Name (optional) and age
4. Repeat for each child

Age is used by the "Today's Pick" recommendation.

### Area filter

Default covers all of Shiga. Settings → Area filter to narrow to your city or region.

---

## 2. Today's Pick

The main feature on the home screen.

1. **Weather** — auto-fetched (with location permission) or manual
2. **Child age** — auto-filled from settings; for multiple children, picks for the youngest
3. **Available time** — "30 min", "1 hour", "half day"

→ One spot is suggested as today's match.

Tap "More options" to expand to 5 candidates.

---

## 3. Spot Detail

Tap any spot to see:

- Photo
- Official website link
- Phone (tap to call)
- Map (opens Google Maps directions)
- Hours / closed days / pricing
- Parking, duration estimate, station proximity
- Dog-friendly status
- Reviews from other parents

### Favorites
Tap the ♡ on the spot detail to save. With an account, favorites sync across devices.

---

## 4. Reviews

### Posting (account required)

1. Spot detail → scroll down → **"💬 Post Review"**
2. Pick the age of the child who came along
3. Rate it: great / okay / not great
4. Optional comment
5. Optional photo (1 image)
6. Sense tags (multi-select): "stroller-friendly", "spacious", "crowded", etc.
7. **"✅ Submit"**

Reviews are anonymous (only @handle shown).

### Edit / Delete

Settings → **"💬 My Reviews"**:
- **Edit** — change body and rating
- **Delete** — remove the review

### Reporting

If you see an inappropriate review, tap the **⚠** button on the right side of that review.
Report goes to: `99letters99@gmail.com`

---

## 5. Submit a Spot (account required)

You can request that a new spot be added.

### Submit

1. Diary tab → **"+ Add new spot"**
2. Name / category / area / address
3. "Register and write a review"

Submitted spots are in "pending" state. Once an admin approves, they go live.

### Edit / Withdraw

Settings → **"📍 My Spot Submissions"**:
- **Pending** submissions: edit / withdraw
- **Rejected** submissions: withdraw (with rejection reason)
- **Approved** submissions: managed by the admin (use the report email to request changes)

## 6. Characters and Footprints

Visiting spots, posting reviews, or completing missions unlocks characters and badges.

### 10 Characters

| Character | Unlock condition |
|---|---|
| Kodo-chan | Default |
| Koto-chan | View 5 spots |
| Biwa-chan | First review post |
| ... | (7 more, see in-app) |

Each has a Bikkuriman-style rarity:
- Green = common
- Pink = rare
- Holo = super rare

Settings → **"Favorite Character"** to choose who appears on home.

### 11 Footprint Badges

Earned by visiting categories of spots:
- "Park Master"
- "Indoor Play Maniac"
- "Café Hopper"
- ... etc.

Settings → **"Footprints with Kodo-chan"** to see progress.

## 7. Account

Account is optional, but unlocks more features.

### Sign up

1. Settings → **"Login / Sign up"**
2. Email + password (6+ chars)
3. Handle (display name)

### Login

Email + password.

### Logout

Settings → Account → **"Logout"**

## 8. Cross-Device (PC ↔ iPhone)

Logged in, your favorites / reviews / unlocked characters sync across all your devices.

- Post a review on iPhone → also visible on PC
- Favorite a spot on PC → same favorites on iPhone

---

## 9. Use Like an App on iPhone (PWA)

1. Open KODOCO in Safari
2. Share button → **"Add to Home Screen"**
3. Confirm name → "Add"

Launching from the home-screen icon shows it full-screen (no address bar).

---

## 10. About Children's Data

- Your children's age, favorites, etc. are stored in your browser (localStorage) by default
- With an account, the same info is also synced to the cloud (visible only to you)
- Reviews are stored anonymously — no personally identifying information
- See [Privacy Policy](https://kodoco.jp/privacy.html)

## 11. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Login state lost after adding to home screen | Log out and log back in |
| Review not appearing | Check connectivity → reload |
| Submitted spot not showing | "Pending" — wait for admin approval |
| Photos not loading | Check connectivity → reload |
| Character not unlocking | Try reloading after meeting the condition |

Anything else: `99letters99@gmail.com`
